# The Glenolia: public facts for a website-impression study

Snapshot prepared 3 November 2026. Refresh these facts from the listed public
pages before a later study. This seed contains no teacher-only learning packs,
subscriber records, child records, personal remarks, credentials or account data.

- Official website: https://theglenolia.com.au/
- The Glenolia is a developing farm destination in Glenorie, New South Wales.
- Its public line is **A place to grow and gather.** The name combines Glenorie
  and magnolia; an established magnolia landscape is part of the project story.
- The vision reflects founder Dr Justin's care for people and shared learning.
  John Ou leads the developing coffee plantation project with consultants.
- The website presents four areas in preparation: café and farm experiences,
  coffee growing, hands-on workshops, and local connections.
- September 2027 is a **target opening**, not a confirmed opening day or a
  current invitation to visit. Visiting details are to be announced.
- The planned café is vegetarian, with eggs, dairy and honey allowed.
- Coffee cultivation is early-stage. The dated 1 November 2026 Paraíso pilot
  owner report records 27 plants at 1 m spacing, average 17 cm height, 26°C,
  soil pH 6.5, and a moisture reading 9 whose unit/scale was not supplied.
  These are dated owner observations, not live weather or harvest evidence.
- The separate IPR107, NR25 and Marsellesa seed-starting report does not establish
  a harvest. NR25's identity remains unresolved.
- Public actions include registering for news and viewing project progress.
  Do not infer that a signup form has been tested from seeing its page.
- The English teacher entry introduces classroom learning materials and a
  verified adult teacher workspace. Farm visits are still in preparation.
  School/education-authority endorsement and complete syllabus coverage are not
  established. The site's language options outside education are separate.
- Pip, Milla and Dot are fictional learning companions: a potted coffee seedling,
  a separate magnolia bud and an earthworm. Their illustrations are not photos
  of completed facilities, actual crops or measured learning outcomes.
